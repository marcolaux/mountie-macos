Mountie 1.3.5 — no more duplicate Finder sidebar entries.

**Fixed in 1.3.5**
- **Finder sidebar duplicates:** WebDAV shares could pile up extra sidebar entries after each remount. Mountie now clears those leftovers, including ones already in your sidebar.

**Install:** download the zip, unzip it, and drag Mountie to ~/Applications (or /Applications).
Notarized; macOS opens it without warnings. Existing installs update themselves.
