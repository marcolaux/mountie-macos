Mountie 1.3.4 — open a share's folder from the menu bar.

**New in 1.3.4**
- **Show in Finder from the menu bar:** the menu bar menu has a new Show in Finder submenu. Pick a mounted share to open its folder in Finder.

**Install:** download the zip, unzip it, and drag Mountie to ~/Applications (or /Applications).
Notarized; macOS opens it without warnings. Existing installs update themselves.
